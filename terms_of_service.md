# SecureBank Financial Analytics — Terms of Service

**Status: Project terms for this educational prototype; obtain legal review before production use.**

## 1. Purpose
SecureBank Financial Analytics is an educational demonstration for exploring transaction analytics and unusual-pattern detection. It is not a bank, financial adviser, fraud determination service, or regulated financial product.

## 2. Data and privacy
CSV files uploaded for analysis are processed by the application server. The app code is designed not to automatically save uploaded CSV files to the MySQL transaction database. Do not upload real customer records, account numbers, payment credentials, or other sensitive personal or financial information. This statement does not replace a full privacy policy or hosting-provider data-processing terms.

## 3. Analytics limitations
Anomaly detection identifies patterns that may warrant review; it does not establish fraud, guarantee accuracy, or replace professional investigation. Demo figures are illustrative. Verify results independently before relying on them.

## 4. Account security
Keep your password and authenticator secret private. If authenticator-based two-factor authentication is enabled, you are responsible for retaining access to the authenticator. This prototype does not currently provide recovery codes or an account recovery flow for lost authenticator access.

## 5. Availability and liability
The prototype is provided for educational use on an as-is and as-available basis. No guarantee is made that it will be uninterrupted, error-free, or suitable for production or compliance-sensitive use.

## 6. Changes and contact
These project terms may be updated as the prototype evolves. No dedicated legal/contact address has been configured for this project.
